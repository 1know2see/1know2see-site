1know2see

这是个人站。看见，是把事情想清楚之后的结果。名字来自一只见二：看见一件事，再看见它的另一面。想得更深，看得更远，把线索收成判断，再把它做出来。界面做清楚，系统做稳，文字留得住。 This is a personal site. Seeing is what remains after the thinking is clear. The name 一只见二 means seeing a thing, then seeing its other side. Think deeper, see further: gather the clues into a judgment, then make it. Clear interfaces, steady systems, words worth staying with.
