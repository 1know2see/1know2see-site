02 — 视线 02 — Sight

想得更深。Think deeper. 看得更远。See further.

1know2see 来自「一只见二」。它既是名字，也是一种看法：先看见眼前的事，再看见它藏着的另一面。工作室因此得名。想把界面做清楚，把系统做稳，把文字留得住。 1know2see comes from 一只见二 — a name, and a way of looking. See what is in front of you, then the side it keeps hidden. The studio is named for that. Clear interfaces, steady systems, words worth staying with.

  1. 01

    深Deeper

    把问题看到底下，而不是停在表面。 Follow the question down, not across the surface.

  2. 02

    远Further

    今天的决定，要经得起下一步。 A decision should still hold at the next step.

  3. 03

    准Precise

    动效、文字与结构，都为意思服务。 Motion, type, and structure serve the meaning.
